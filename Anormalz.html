Anormalz

Concept

I decided to create a fictional planet from scratch, purely out of curiosity and research into how a planet works, and I ended up creating a planet called Extremis. At another point in my life, I also took a comic book course, where I created a robot that observed the human race and criticized them for their way of being, especially in relation to the world they live in.

When I got to university, I began combining ideas and started writing a trilogy called Anormalz. The trilogy is divided into three books:

Destroied;

Abandoned;

Conquered.

All three criticize humanity, especially the generations of the 21st century and the transition from the 20th century, as well as their customs. The first book addresses recklessness, selfishness, and arelessness. The second addresses misinformation, pride, and disorganization. The third addresses prejudice, extremist tendencies, and ignorance.

Tools and Tecnics

This work is mostly literary, with some artwork representing the author’s interpretation of the story, created in Blender and/or Photoshop.
