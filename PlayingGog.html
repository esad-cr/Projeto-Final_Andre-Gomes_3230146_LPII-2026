Playing god

Concept

Initially, tarot cards were created for games; the tarot suits later evolved into the playing cards we know today. Wands became clubs, swords remained swords, cups became hearts (although in English the term ‘cups’ was kept in tarot), and coins, or pentacles in some versions, became diamonds.

With the intention of using the tarot suits and major arcana, I created a board game that blends the newer meaning of ‘destiny’ associated with tarot with its original purpose of being playable. This combination reminded me of the political state of the world and the struggle for resources among ‘World Leaders,’ as they play with the destiny of humankind as if they were gods an irresponsible fight for power, just like in this game, where players use cards that symbolize destiny

This game works with both small and large groups, as the intention is to allow as many players as possible to participate, avoiding those situations where the game doesn’t accommodate everyone and players are forced to form teams.

Tools and Tecnics

The graphic part of this game was created in Illustrator and printed on 300 gram paper.
