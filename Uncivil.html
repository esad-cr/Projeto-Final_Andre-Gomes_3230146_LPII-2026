Uncivil

Concept

This is an RPG for heroes and villains, with a strong emphasis on creativity and imagination, supported by rules that give ideas room to breathe. The game uses a direct and intuitive dice based progression system as the skill level mechanic, along with a simple and straightforward combat system. This RPG has no classes, allowing for greater freedom of role and removing the need for multiclassing to perform multiple functions.

This game emerged from my dissatisfaction with the D&D system and the feeling that other systems still weren’t enough. The game focuses on a system designed for superheroes, since I’ve always been a huge fan of them ever since I was a child and I even created several of my own.

Tools and Tecnics

The layout was done in InDesign, and the cover, back cover, and spine were created in Illustrator and printed on 300 gram paper.
