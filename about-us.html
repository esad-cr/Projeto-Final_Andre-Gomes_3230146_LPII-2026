About Us

Who are we

We are a new team that emerged in Portugal with the intention of creating new experiences for various types of audiences. These experiences are delivered by us in the form of games, digital or physical, scripts, books and animation.

Origames was created in late 2025 with 2 different projects, Playing God and Uncivil. Today we have more projects made by our fantastic and dedicated members. The team is still growing, so you have the chance to become the next member of our team.

We currently have no physical headquarters, but you can contact us through social media, by our email or by others.

What are our goals

Life is uncertain, so our goal is to give everyone as many experiences as possible through our projects.

We also hope that with this work we can change the world, even if just a little.
