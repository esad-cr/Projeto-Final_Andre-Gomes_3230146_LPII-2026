What is this website?

This website is part of a project I created for the Project Laboratory course in the Graphic and Multimedia Design degree at ESAD.CR (Escola Superior de Artes e Design das Caldas da Rainha), In this project, I had to demonstrate my knowledge of programming languages, in this case, I used HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, along with additional Unreal Engine nodes for my game.

Beyond the technical component, this project also required me to showcase my understanding of UX/UI, accessibility, and interaction design. I focused on creating a functional, intuitive, and inclusive experience that reflects both my design approach and my ability to integrate different areas of digital media into a cohesive final product.

Who Am I?

I am a Multimedia student at ESAD.CR, specializing in UX. I am currently using this company style portfolio website to showcase my work while I develop a more polished and market ready professional website.

This temporary platform allows me to present my projects, document my creative and technical process, and demonstrate my skills in design, interaction, and digital media. As I continue to grow as a designer, this space serves both as a portfolio and as a record of my evolution in the field.

What kind of works will I find here?

Here you can expect to find projects related to game development (both physical and digital), worldbuilding, and animation.

This website is continuously growing, and in the future you will be able to find an even wider variety of work, reflecting my evolution as a designer and creator.
