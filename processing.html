Playing God Digital Demo

This is a digital version of the game Playing God created by Origames. This JavaScript version of the game is intended to give players the basic experience of the game before purchasing it. Some rules and cards were adapted for a version prepared for two players.

Warning the demo is in PT-PT!

Rules

The player must play cards in ascending order (from lowest to highest), with the order being 2 < 3 < 4 < 5 < 6 < 7 < 8 < 9 < 10 < N < P < Q < K < A (A's resets the game) < God cards

There are two types of cards, God cards and normal cards. The God cards have special abilitys that can turn the game, normal cards are just normal

On a player’s turn, they may choose either to play a card or to draw a card. The round ends when no one can play any more cards, sending the cards to the Past. After several rounds, the winner is the player whose number of cards in the Past is exactly 13 or as close as possible (as long as it is above 13).

There are three zones in the game (4 counting your hand). The future, where the player grabs cards. The present where the player plays the cards. The Past, where the player discards the cards after a round.
